# Third-party brain mesh

`brain_mesh.obj` / `brain_mesh.npz` are derived from:

> "Human brain, Cerebrum & Brainstem" (https://skfb.ly/6SDzJ) by FrankJohansson
> licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
> (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: exported geometry-only Wavefront OBJ (via SAGE project intermediate),
centered, Y-up normalized (brainstem toward −Y), decimated for HUD wireframe,
occupancy grid baked for interior filament constraints.
